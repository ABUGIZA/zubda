Changelog

What’s new in Zubda

Every version and what changed in it, in plain words. Updates reach you inside the app, signed and tested.

1.3.3 26 October 2026

Updates reach you right away

  • Easier

    Updates reach you right away

    As soon as a new version is out it shows up, whether you just opened Zubda or it was already open.

  • Clearer

    One clear name

    The Zubda name shows once, at the top of the window, in a clear type.

1.3.2 26 October 2026

Small improvements

  • Sturdier

    Small improvements

    Zubda is sturdier under the hood.

1.3.1 26 October 2026

Updates come to you

  • Easier

    Updates come to you

    As soon as a new version is out, its box opens in the middle of the app with an «Update now» button, and an «Update now» button stays in the top bar. No trip to Settings.

  • Clearer

    «Upload a video», clearly

    The small icon beside the link is now a button that says «Upload a video», for a video saved on your computer instead of a link. You can also drag it into the app.

1.3.0 25 October 2026

«Explain it»: the video as if you’d watched it

  • New

    «Explain it»

    Once the report is done, Zubda writes a short explanation of the video as if you'd watched it, technical or not. Every point carries its time, and a click opens the video at that moment.

  • Clearer

    Each video drawn the way it reads best

    Steps to tick when there's something to do, who's tied to whom when there are sides, cause and effect, a claim and its evidence, and the code line by line when it's on screen.

  • Sturdier

    Nothing made up

    Code, figures and times in the explanation must come from the video itself; anything without a source in it is left out.

  • Easier

    On or off in Settings

    «Explain each video by itself» is on from the start and uses some of your plan's limit. Turned off, or on an older video, you ask for it in one click.

1.2.3 25 October 2026

Always opens, safer and lighter

  • Sturdier

    Zubda always opens

    The daily download-engine update ran while the app was open and could stop halfway, so Zubda wouldn't open. It now lands in its own folder, and if a program file goes missing, Zubda puts it back by itself and opens.

  • Sturdier

    Safe from web pages

    A page open in your browser can't reach Zubda on your computer: it can't read your library, sign you out or delete anything.

  • Lighter

    Big files, lighter

    Adding a video from your computer no longer takes as much memory as the file, and no copy stays once it's read. Copies older versions left behind are cleared by themselves.

1.2.2 21 October 2026

Listening works on Windows set to Arabic

  • Sturdier

    Listening works on Windows set to Arabic

    On computers with Windows set to Arabic, a video stopped at «Listen» with a 'utf-8' codec error at the first Arabic word. Fixed: press «Carry on» on that video and it carries on from the same files.

1.2.1 21 October 2026

Send works, and the essence in your language

  • Sturdier

    Send always works

    In the «Chat» look it could stop after changing the thinking level or a little while after opening the app. What you type no longer clears itself either.

  • Easier

    The essence in your language, in one press

    If it comes out in another language, a button under it writes it again from the same files, without downloading or listening, and the next videos follow.

  • Clearer

    Ask for it in the chat

    «In English please» brings up the same button, and answers come in the language of your question. Pasting a link read in another language writes it again in yours.

  • Sturdier

    Sturdier settings

    Fixed a rare error when pressing settings quickly.

1.2.0 21 October 2026

Lighter, faster, and no plan needed

  • Lighter

    Opens in three seconds, not ten

    And drops back to half the memory as soon as a video is done, because the listening engine now closes itself when it finishes.

  • Lighter

    A speech model half the size

    On computers without an NVIDIA card: 0.8 GB instead of 1.6, with the same quality.

  • New

    A new interface, in two looks

    «Tabs» (the default) and «Chat», to pick from in Settings.

  • New

    No subscription needed

    A free ChatGPT account works smoothly on the Luna model, which now thinks deeper. The welcome explains what to do if you have no account.

  • New

    Or no account at all

    It downloads the video, writes down every word and catches the scenes on your computer. The essence waits until you connect an account, and carries on from where it stopped.

  • Clearer

    The connected account, in plain sight

    Its email and plan always show, and you can switch accounts or sign out in one press, without signing out of Claude Code or Codex.

  • Easier

    Updating is a button in the app

    Press it, it downloads and installs itself, and your library is never touched.

  • Sturdier

    Clearer errors

    Error messages give the real cause, and a rare crash that could close the app is fixed.

1.1.0 19 October 2026

Several videos, and faster

  • New

    Several videos at once

    Each video in its own tab, from any site. Two run at once by default; the rest wait their turn.

  • Faster

    The same video in about half the time

    It listens and watches at the same time, and writes the essence in pieces. Measured: 6:10 → 3:14.

  • New

    Pick the model

    And the thinking level and fast mode. The top of the window shows which model is running.

  • Clearer

    A clearer essence

    Brief or full, a copy button on every section, and the full transcript with search.

  • Clearer

    Connecting shows every step

    As it happens, with a button to open the sign-in page again.

  • Sturdier

    Your plan’s limit

    If you reach it, Zubda pauses and carries on by itself; unfinished videos carry on when you open Zubda again.

  • Easier

    The first run asks

    For your language and your model, with the default already picked.

1.0.0 18 October 2026

The first release

  • First release

    Paste a link, get the essence

    From YouTube, X or TikTok, or add a video file.

  • First release

    On your own plan

    Runs on your Claude or ChatGPT plan, with no API key.

  • First release

    Ask and export

    Ask your whole library, and export the essence as Markdown or PDF.

How updates reach you

An “Update ready” button appears at the top of the window. It tells you what’s new, then installs and reopens Zubda in seconds. Every update is signed, and your library and settings are untouched.
