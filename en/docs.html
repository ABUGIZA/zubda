Install

One installer, no admin rights, and it sets up the rest by itself.

Steps

  1. Download the installer

    Download Zubda-Setup.exe from the download button at the top of the page.

  2. Run it, and get past the Windows warning

    Windows shows a SmartScreen warning because Zubda has no paid signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Windows protected your PC

    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting. Running this app might put your PC at risk.

    More info
    App: Zubda-Setup.exe
    Publisher: Unknown publisher
    Run anywayDon’t run

  3. Let it set up

    The first time, it downloads the app’s engine (about 400 MB), which takes a few minutes depending on your connection.

  4. Open Zubda

    From the Start menu or the desktop.

First run

When you first open Zubda, it downloads two engines by itself, and you can watch their progress in Settings:

Listening engine
About 0.8 GB, or 1.6 GB on a computer with an NVIDIA card. It listens to the video and writes down what is said.
YouTube engine
Fetches videos from YouTube and the other sites.
Good to know

If an engine’s download fails, you can retry it from Settings → Engines.

Security

You sign in on the company’s own page, and your password never passes through Zubda.

After connecting

The email of the account that got connected shows with its plan, and a question: Is it the right one? Check it, especially if you have more than one account. Then you pick the model; the default is already selected.

Switch account or sign out

The connected account’s email and plan always show in Settings → Account. Connected an account other than the one you pay with? Press Switch account. The sign-in page opens on the account that’s open in your browser, so if you want another, open chatgpt.com or claude.ai and switch there first, then come back and press Continue.

Good to know

Sign out signs Zubda out only. Your account in the browser, and Claude Code or Codex if you have them, are untouched, and your library stays as it is.

Without an account

You can use Zubda with no account at all, in part: it downloads the video, writes down every word with its time, and catches every scene the screen shows. All of it on your computer. What takes understanding is done by the AI in your account, so it stays locked until you connect:

  • The essence and the key points

    The AI writes them: it understands the words and the pictures, then picks what matters and puts it in order.

  • Is it worth your time?

    Judging it takes understanding the whole video, not just hearing it.

  • Reading the screens

    The code, numbers and links that appeared are read and explained by the AI.

  • Ask the video and your library

    An answer takes understanding your question and finding it in the words and scenes.

Once you connect an account, open the video and press Finish the essence now: it carries on from where it stopped, without downloading or hearing the video again.

GuideUsing Zubda

Read the essence

One report that tells you whether it’s worth watching, what’s in it, and where.

The reel

Above the report is a reel of all the video’s frames and sound, with the filler folded into pleats. Hover it to see the frame and the fact at any second; click anywhere to jump to that point.

What the report holds

  1. The verdict

    Worth watching, partly worth it, or the essence is enough. With a usefulness score out of 10.

  2. The essence

    The whole video in a few lines.

  3. Key points

    Each point with its time and one idea per line, then the steps, the tools and the links.

  4. Shown on screen, never said

    Code, settings and text with their frame and exact wording. Frames appear only when they add something the words don’t.

  5. Watch these yourself

    The only moments where words don’t replace the picture.

  6. Full transcript

    With its times and a search field: type a word and see where it was said.

Good to know

Every time you see is a link that opens the original video at that second. And every section has a copy button.

Brief or full

Choose above the report, and Zubda remembers your choice:

Brief

The verdict and the essence in a few lines. For deciding fast.

Full

Everything: points, steps, frames and the full transcript.

GuideUsing Zubda

Explain it

A short explanation of the video as if you’d watched it, technical or not, with each part drawn the way it reads best.

What it is

Above the report are two buttons: Explain it and Every detail. «Explain it» opens first, with the video explained as if you’d watched it. Every point carries its time, and a click opens the video at that moment. «Every detail» holds the full report described in Read the essence.

Beside the parts that need a picture is a frame from the video itself, as proof of what’s said. Press Enlarge to see it big.

Each part drawn the way it reads best

Zubda works out what kind of video it is and how its ideas connect, then draws each part in the shape that fits it. Among the shapes:

  1. The steps

    A list to tick, when there’s something to do.

  2. Who is tied to whom

    The sides and how they connect, when there are sides.

  3. Cause and effect

    What led to what.

  4. The claim and its case

    The opinion and the evidence the video gives for it, when it argues something.

  5. The code

    Line by line when code is on screen, with a copy button.

  6. Comparisons, figures and events

    Tables, comparisons and charts for the numbers, and a timeline of what happened.

Nothing made up

Code, figures and times in the explanation must come from the video itself; anything without a source in it is left out.

When it is written

Once the report is done, Zubda writes the explanation while you read. It usually takes 1 to 3 minutes, and the full report is ready under Every detail meanwhile.

The setting
Explain each video by itself in Settings → Analysis. On from the start.
An older video, or the setting off
Press Explain the video and it writes it for you.
A question?
Under the explanation is Ask anything about the video.

Settings

Five sections, and everything in its place.

The sections

  • Account

    Connect Claude or ChatGPT and choose the one in use. The meter at the top shows how much of your plan’s limit is left and when it renews.

  • Language

    The app’s language, and the essence’s own language: read an English video’s essence in Arabic.

  • Model

    The model, the thinking level, fast mode, and how many videos run at once.

  • Reading

    Using the video’s own captions, and keeping the video file.

  • Engines

    The YouTube engine, the listening engine and the graphics-card speed-up. They download by themselves, and any one can be retried.

Updates

Updates reach you inside the app, and install in one click.

How it reaches you

  1. The «What’s new» box opens

    In the middle of the app, the first time you open it after a new version is out: what changed, and an «Update now» button.

  2. You press Update now

    In the box, or on the button that stays at the top of the app if you chose «Later».

  3. It installs and reopens

    In seconds. Your library and settings are untouched.

Signed and tested

Security

Every update is digitally signed with Zubda’s key, and the app checks the signature and the file’s fingerprint before installing. Anything that doesn’t match is refused.

And an update only reaches you once it is published complete and tested.

GuideHelp

FAQ

The questions we get most, answered briefly.

Does it only read the captions?

No. It takes the video’s words (from its own captions, or by listening itself) and watches its frames, so it catches what was on screen but never said: code, settings, numbers and text.

What is «Explain it»?

A short explanation of the video as if you’d watched it, written by Zubda after the report. Each part is drawn the way it reads best, and every point carries its time. The full guide is here.

Can I jump to a moment in the video?

Yes. Every time in the report and the explanation is a link that opens the original video at that second, and the reel above the report takes you to any point you click. The full transcript has a search field that shows where any word was said.

Which sites does it take?

YouTube, X, TikTok, Instagram, Vimeo and more, plus playlist and channel links, and video or audio files on your computer.

I don’t pay for anything. Does it work?

Yes. Connect a free ChatGPT account (or make one at chatgpt.com with your email) and Zubda works in full on the Luna model. A subscription gives stronger models and a bigger limit, but it isn’t required.

I connected the wrong account. How do I switch?

In Settings → Account, press “Switch account”. The sign-in page opens on the account that’s open in your browser, so switch to the right one on the site first. The account’s email and plan always show, so you know which one is connected.

Can I use it with no account at all?

Yes, in part: it downloads the video, writes down every word and catches the scenes on your computer. The essence, the key points and asking the video need the AI, so they stay locked until you connect an account. A free ChatGPT account is enough.

How much of my plan does it use?

It depends on the video’s length. A fifteen-minute video takes a small part of Claude Max’s five-hour limit. The meter at the top always shows what’s left.

I have no NVIDIA card. Does it work?

Yes. If the video has captions (most of YouTube) it reads them directly. If not, it listens on the processor: slower, but it works.

How long does it take?

A fifteen-minute video takes about three minutes with Claude, depending on your computer, the model and the thinking level. You can open several tabs and keep working.

I hit my plan’s limit mid-way?

Nothing is lost. Zubda pauses, shows when the limit comes back, and carries on by itself then; just keep it open. And if you shut your computer down, unfinished videos carry on as soon as you open Zubda.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

Windows 10 and 11 only, for now.

Private or paid videos?

Zubda reads public videos. For a private one, download it the legitimate way and drag the file onto the window.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Zubda → Uninstall. Your library stays in its folder in case you come back; delete the folder if you don’t want it.

I found a problem

Zubda tells you on screen what happened and what to do next. If that doesn’t solve it, report it here.
